GoT House Quiz is an unofficial collection of eight original personality quizzes inspired by Game of Thrones and House of the Dragon. All questions and result descriptions are written for this site. Decorative illustrations are original.

Choices become affinities

Each quiz has ten questions and four choices per question. An answer contributes three points to its main match and one point to a secondary match. For each result, we divide the points earned by the highest total that result could earn across the quiz. This gives results with different numbers of scoring opportunities a fairer comparison.

The largest normalized score wins. If results tie, the listed result order breaks the tie and the screen explains that the runner-up tied. This is a simple entertainment model, not a scientifically validated personality test. We do not present the score as a probability or romantic compatibility percentage.

The Dance is a separate question

The allegiance quiz has two possible results: the Blacks and the Greens. Its questions explore the competing succession arguments. A five-to-five answer split gives a tie, shown on the results screen. Your house or character result does not determine your allegiance.

Our approach to the stories

We use recognizable character, house, and dragon names. The personality matches are subjective fan readings. Dragon compatibility in particular is imagined rather than a claim of established lore. We avoid book-only plot reveals, story-ending details, and hidden-parentage revelations.

For official information, visit HBO’s Game of Thrones page or the official House of the Dragon dragon index.
