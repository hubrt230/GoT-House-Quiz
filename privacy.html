Your choices stay with you.

Quiz answers are calculated in your browser. This site does not send your answers to a server or collect a name, email address, or account.

Remembering your progress

Session storage saves quiz progress in this browser tab. Retaking or starting fresh resets that quiz’s answers. Closing a tab normally clears this storage, though a browser restoring a previous session may preserve it. The quiz still works when storage is unavailable; progress simply may not survive a reload.

Sharing and saving results

Sharing passes only your result name and a public result link to the app you choose. Individual answers are not included. The Save result card button generates an image in your browser. Neither action posts anything automatically.

Hosting requests

This site has no analytics, ads, tracking cookies, or third-party font requests. The hosting provider can process ordinary web requests under its own privacy policy. If hosted on GitHub Pages, see GitHub’s privacy statement. External links and sharing apps have their own policies.

This notice should be updated if the site later adds analytics, advertising, forms, or other services.
